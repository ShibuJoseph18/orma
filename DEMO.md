# Record Orma

Open http://localhost:5173/?demo=1 and refresh before recording.

1. Click Connect sources. Keep all four platforms selected.
2. Click Ingest selected platforms. The animation takes about seven seconds.
3. Explore the actual knowledge graph, then click Ask your library.
4. Ask: What have I saved about software engineering career?
5. Show source cards from all four platforms. Click a citation and expand See how Orma found this.

The import animation is a demo replay of the existing library. The graph and search results are real FalkorDB retrieval. Recording search skips new Gemini calls to stay fast. Live ingestion and Gemini question interpretation remain available at http://localhost:5173/?live=1.

Current library: LinkedIn 100, Instagram 40, YouTube Watch Later 50, X 50. Instagram returned 40 saves. Do not claim 50 Instagram items or complete video analysis for every source. Each resource states its analysis coverage.
